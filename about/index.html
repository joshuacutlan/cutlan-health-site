About

Hi, I'm Josh.

I'm an independent agent based in Odessa, on the north side of Tampa Bay, licensed in Florida and 18 other states. Most of my clients are shopping for a plan on their own, and the question I answer most is whether the plan they have is costing them more than it should.

Last updated October 21, 2026

How I got here

I'm originally from Illinois and now call Florida home, where I live with my family. I got into health insurance through my brother, who's been in the industry for years, and it clicked fast. I saw firsthand how much the right guidance matters when someone's staring at a wall of confusing plans, prices and fine print.

When I'm not helping clients, I'm a die-hard hockey fan and I travel every chance I get.

Who usually calls me

Most calls start with one of these:

  • Employees paying too much at work. Work plans charge a healthy 25-year-old the same as a 55-year-old coworker. Sometimes that's still the better deal. Sometimes it isn't. Here's how to tell.
  • 1099 and self-employed. Nobody hands you a plan anymore. Your options, spelled out.
  • People turning 26. Leaving a parent's plan without a single uninsured day. What to do and when.
  • Young couples. Adding a spouse at work can cost thousands a year. How to check yours.

What working with me looks like

It starts with a call, five to ten minutes. I ask why you're shopping, who you see now, what has to be covered, and how much you lean on a plan in a normal year. With that, I pull the private and Marketplace plans built for the way you use coverage, and we go over them on a screen share, so you see the same plans, networks and prices I do instead of a list of numbers read over the phone. Private plans ask health questions and can say no, so they aren't for everyone, and you'll know early if one isn't a fit. When your work plan or the Marketplace beats anything I sell, that's the recommendation you get, and I earn nothing on that advice. That's the point of hiring an independent agent.

My cell is the business line, so a text reaches me, not an office. Replies usually come within a few hours: Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 8 PM, Friday 9 AM to 7:30 PM, and weekends 10 AM to 3 PM, Eastern.

You never get a bill from me. The carrier pays my commission, and what you pay is exactly what you'd pay buying direct. The full breakdown of who pays me.
