About

Hi, I'm Josh.

I'm a licensed health insurance agent in the Tampa area. I help people in their 20s and 30s figure out whether they're paying too much for health insurance, and what to do about it.

Last updated October 11, 2026

How I got here

I'm originally from Illinois and now call Florida home, where I live with my family. I got into health insurance through my brother, who's been in the industry for years, and it clicked fast. I saw firsthand how much the right guidance matters when someone's staring at a wall of confusing plans, prices and fine print.

When I'm not helping clients, I'm a die-hard hockey fan and I travel every chance I get.

Who I help

Most of the people I work with are in their 20s and 30s, and most of them have never had anyone explain how their health insurance is priced:

  • Employees paying too much at work. Work plans charge a healthy 25-year-old the same as a 55-year-old coworker. Sometimes that's still the better deal. Sometimes it isn't. Here's how to tell.
  • Self-employed and 1099 workers. Nobody hands you a plan anymore. Your options, in plain English.
  • People turning 26. Aging off a parent's plan without a gap. What to do and when.
  • Young couples. Adding a spouse at work can cost thousands a year. How to check yours.

How I work

I compare private plans and Marketplace options side by side and tell you which one wins for you. Private plans ask health questions and can say no, so they're not for everyone, and I'll tell you upfront if one isn't a fit. If your work plan or the Marketplace is the better deal, I'll tell you that, even though I don't get paid for it. That honesty is the whole point.

I answer my own phone and texts, usually the same day: Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 8 PM, Friday 9 AM to 7:30 PM, and weekends 10 AM to 3 PM, Eastern.

Working with me is free. The insurance carrier pays me, and the price is the same as going direct. Here's exactly how I'm paid.
