Pay, explained

How I'm paid (and why it costs you nothing)

It's a fair question: if working with me is free, how do I make a living? Here's the honest answer.

Last updated October 14, 2026
The short answer

You don't pay me. When you enroll in a plan through me, the insurance carrier pays me a commission. The plan costs you the same as if you'd bought it direct, so working with me is free. If the best answer for you is a plan I don't get paid on, like staying on your work plan, I'll still tell you.

Where the money comes from

Insurance carriers pay licensed agents to find, enroll and look after their members. That pay comes from the carrier, not from you. Carriers build it into the plan's price whether or not you use an agent, so it's never added on top as a fee, and you'd pay the same price for the same plan without me.

Why that matters to you

Because I'm not charging you, you can ask me anything, compare as many options as you want, and walk away if none of them fit. There's nothing to sign and no cost to finding out.

The honest part

Commissions aren't the same on every plan, and some good answers pay me nothing at all. If you're better off keeping your work plan, or your income gets you a big Marketplace subsidy, that's what I'll tell you. I show you the numbers side by side and explain why one wins, so you never have to take my word for it.

My bet is simple: if I give people the straight answer, they come back when things change and send their friends.

What you get for free

  • A side-by-side comparison of your realistic options
  • A plain-English explanation of what's covered and what isn't
  • Help applying and lining up a start date with no gap
  • Someone who answers when you have a claim question or your life changes

Questions I get

Do I pay you anything?+

No. I don't charge fees. If you enroll in a plan through me, the insurance carrier pays me.

Will my plan cost more because I used an agent?+

No. The plan costs the same whether you buy it from the carrier, on the Marketplace, or through me. Carriers build agent pay into the plan's price whether or not you use an agent, so you pay the same either way.

Do some plans pay you more than others?+

Yes, commissions vary by carrier and plan, and some options, like staying on your work plan, pay me nothing. That's why I show you the comparison and the reasons, so you can check my thinking.
