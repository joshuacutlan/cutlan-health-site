Straight answers

Questions people ask me

Short, honest answers. If yours isn't here, text me.

How much does it cost to work with a health insurance agent?+

Nothing. It's free to work with me — insurance carriers build the agent's compensation into the plan whether or not you use one, so you pay the same premium either way. You just get someone comparing your options and answering your calls.

Can I get health insurance if I'm self-employed or 1099?+

Absolutely — that's who I help most. Self-employed people, 1099 contractors, and freelancers have two main paths: ACA Marketplace plans (guaranteed acceptance, possible income-based subsidies) and private PPO plans (often lower cost with broader networks for healthy applicants). I'll compare both for your situation.

I'm young and healthy. Am I overpaying for my employer's health insurance?+

Maybe. At most jobs, everyone on the same plan pays the same paycheck deduction whether they're 24 or 60, and adding a spouse often costs a lot more. A private plan priced on your age and health can cost less for some young, healthy people. You'd give up your employer's share and the pre-tax deduction, and private plans can decline applicants or exclude pre-existing conditions, so I compare the real numbers before you change anything.

What's the difference between private health insurance and the ACA Marketplace?+

ACA Marketplace plans accept everyone, cover pre-existing conditions, and offer income-based subsidies, but are often HMOs limited to open enrollment. Private PPO plans are medically underwritten — you can be declined — but for healthy applicants they often cost less, offer broad nationwide networks, and can be bought year-round.

Can I enroll any time, or only during open enrollment?+

ACA Marketplace plans generally require open enrollment (about Nov 1 to mid-January) unless you have a qualifying life event like losing coverage. Private medically-underwritten plans can be applied for year-round.
