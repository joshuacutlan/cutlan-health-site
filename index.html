Common questions

How much does it cost to work with you? +

Nothing. It's free — carriers build the agent's compensation into the plan whether or not you use one, so you pay the same premium either way. You just get someone comparing your options and answering your calls.

Can I get coverage if I'm self-employed or 1099? +

Absolutely — that's who I help most. You have two paths: ACA Marketplace (guaranteed acceptance, possible subsidies) and private PPO plans (often lower cost with broader networks for healthy applicants). I'll compare both for you.

What's the difference between private and ACA Marketplace? +

Marketplace plans accept everyone, cover pre-existing conditions, and offer income-based subsidies, but are often HMOs limited to open enrollment. Private PPO plans are underwritten — you can be declined — but for healthy applicants often cost less, with broad networks and year-round enrollment.

Can I enroll any time or only during open enrollment? +

ACA Marketplace generally requires open enrollment (about Nov 1–mid-Jan) unless you have a qualifying life event. Private plans can be applied for year-round — helpful if you missed the window.
