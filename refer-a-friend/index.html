Refer a friend

Send a friend my way

Most of my clients first heard about me from someone they trust. If you know a friend, coworker or sibling who's overpaying, turning 26, or just started working for themselves, here's how to send them over.

Last updated October 21, 2026

Good people to send my way

  • Friends in their 20s and 30s paying a lot for a work plan, or for a spouse on it
  • Anyone self-employed, 1099, or driving for the apps
  • Anyone whose 26th birthday will end their parent's coverage
  • Anyone between jobs with a COBRA letter in hand

My license covers 19 states. If they live somewhere else, I'll hand them to a licensed agent I know personally.

What they can expect

  • A quick conversation about what they have now and what they pay.
  • Their choices priced in one table.
  • A yes or no, even when the answer is "keep what you have."
  • No charge, and I only follow up if they ask me to.

A note on rewards

I don't pay for referrals or offer gifts for them. Insurance rules limit that, and I'd rather earn the referral by taking good care of whoever you send. Thank you for trusting me with your people.
